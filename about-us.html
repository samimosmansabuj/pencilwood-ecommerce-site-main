Our Story

Pencilwood was born from a simple idea: that everyday objects should be both beautiful and functional. We set out to bring premium-quality wooden decor and lifestyle accessories to Bangladesh — products that blend elegant craftsmanship with the practical needs of the modern professional.

What started as a small passion project quickly grew into a trusted brand, driven by a commitment to quality materials, honest pricing, and genuine customer care. Every product in our collection is thoughtfully designed to add a touch of warmth and sophistication to your workspace, home, and daily routine.

Today, Pencilwood serves customers across Bangladesh — from Dhaka to Chittagong to every corner of the country — delivering premium goods right to your doorstep, with the care and reliability you deserve.
